Our story

Three decades in the neighbourhood

William McGowan Lettings Ltd is a well-established, family-run property services company based in North West London. Founded 29 years ago by our Director and Owner, William McGowan, the business has built a reputation for professionalism, integrity and exceptional local expertise.

Local expertise

We live where we work

Our sales, lettings and property management team all live within the communities we serve, and have been managing properties — both from our own portfolio and on behalf of other landlords — for over 30 years.

With a combined industry experience of more than 70 years, we offer unrivalled knowledge of North West London, helping clients make informed property decisions with confidence.
